THE 4-HOUSE SYSTEM

Every student is assigned to one of our four houses, fostering teamwork, camaraderie, and spirited inter-house competitions:

BLUE HOUSE

Motto: Wisdom & Truth. Champions of Inter-House Quiz & Science Exhibition.

GREEN HOUSE

Motto: Harmony & Growth. Winners of Campus Eco & Cleanliness Drives.

RED HOUSE

Motto: Courage & Valor. Reigning Annual Sports Day & Athletics Trophy.

YELLOW HOUSE

Motto: Enlightenment & Joy. Winners of Annual Cultural & Speech Contests.

CLUBS & CO-CURRICULAR SOCIETIES

SCIENCE & INNOVATION CLUB

Hands-on model building, scientific exhibitions, and National Science Day project mentorship.

LITERARY & DEBATE CLUB

Elocution, debates, poetry recitation, and essay writing in English, Hindi, and Urdu.

ARTS & CRAFTS GUILD

Painting, rangoli, pottery, origami, and annual wall magazine design exhibitions.

SPORTS & MARTIAL FITNESS

Cricket tournaments, football league, athletics, sprint relays, and daily fitness drills.

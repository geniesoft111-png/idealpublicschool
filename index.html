WHY CHOOSE IDEAL PUBLIC SCHOOL ARANDA?

Founded on 24th December 2004 under the visionary leadership of Ideal Educational and Welfare Trust, Ideal Public School Aranda has grown into a beacon of educational excellence in Siwan, Bihar. Guided by our motto "From Darkness to Light", we blend rigorous academic fundamentals with character building, discipline, and community values.

With bilingual instruction (English, Hindi, and Urdu), affordable fees, government RTE recognition, and a proven track record of 100% board outcomes, we provide the ideal foundation for every child's lifelong success.

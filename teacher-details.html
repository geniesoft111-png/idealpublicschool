About The Director

With more than two decades of dedicated service to education in Bihar, Arshi Kabir has guided Ideal Public School Aranda from its founding in 2004 into one of the most respected academic institutions in Hasanpura and Siwan.

His educational vision centers on creating an environment where progressive 21st-century learning meets enduring moral values, preparing children to excel in competitive board examinations while developing compassion, social ethics, and leadership.

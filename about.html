WELCOME TO IDEAL PUBLIC SCHOOL ARANDA

Ideal Public School Aranda was established on 24th December 2004 under the auspices of the Ideal Educational and Welfare Trust in Aranda, Hasanpura, District Siwan (Bihar). For over two decades, our institution has been dedicated to delivering holistic, values-based, and forward-looking education.

Operating under government recognition (Reg. RTE/SWN/2014-15 0079 and 21812452021924143959), we offer education from Nursery to Class VIII in English, Hindi, and Urdu mediums. Guided by our revered motto "From Darkness to Light" (तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय), we strive to empower every student with knowledge, character, and empathy.

HOLISTIC ATHLETICS & PHYSICAL EDUCATION

At Ideal Public School Aranda, physical education is an integral pillar of our educational ethos. We believe that true learning encompasses mental sharpness, physical agility, teamwork, and sportsman spirit. Under the guidance of trained physical instructors, every student participates in scheduled daily outdoor and indoor sports.

Sports & Games Disciplines

Outdoor Sports

  • Cricket: Pitch play, batting, bowling, and inter-house matches
  • Football: Field training, passing drills, and mini-leagues
  • Volleyball: Standard court play & agility drills
  • Kabaddi & Kho-Kho: Traditional Indian endurance and agility sports
  • Track & Field Athletics: 100m/200m sprints, relay races, and long jump

Indoor & Mind Games

  • Badminton: Singles and doubles tournament coaching
  • Table Tennis: Reflex and coordination training
  • Chess: Strategic thinking and cognitive tournaments
  • Carrom: Recreational and focused indoor play
  • Yoga & Pranayama: Daily morning breathing exercises and flexibility asanas

Annual Sports Meet & March Past

Every academic session culminates in the Annual Inter-House Sports Championship where the Blue, Green, Red, and Gold houses compete across sprints, relays, tug-of-war, and team sports. Our disciplined student parade and flag march during national festivals showcase precision marching, leadership, and national pride.

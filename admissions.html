ADMISSION PROCESS & GUIDELINES

We welcome parents seeking quality, values-based education to enroll their children at Ideal Public School Aranda for the academic session 2026-27. Admissions are open from Nursery to Class VIII.

STEP 1: INQUIRY & REGISTRATION

Obtain the admission registration form from the school office or fill out the online enquiry form below. Basic pupil and parent information is recorded.

STEP 2: DOCUMENT VERIFICATION

Submit required documents (Birth certificate, Transfer Certificate, 4 passport photos, and Aadhaar card) for verification by the admissions committee.

STEP 3: INTERACTION SESSION

An informal, friendly interaction session with the child and parents to understand the student's background and foundational readiness.

STEP 4: ENROLLMENT CONFIRMATION

Upon selection, fee payment confirms the seat. An admission kit containing uniform guidelines, syllabus, and booklist is handed over.

RTE ACT 2009 — 25% FREE SEATS RESERVATION

Under Section 12(1)(c) of the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act 2009 and Government of Bihar notification (Registration No. RTE/SWN/2014-15 0079), Ideal Public School Aranda reserves 25% of all entry seats free of tuition fees for children belonging to economically weaker sections and disadvantaged groups.

Required for RTE Admission: Income certificate / BPL ration card, caste certificate (if applicable), proof of residence within Hasanpura/Aranda, child's birth certificate, and Aadhaar card. Apply directly at our school administrative office or online.

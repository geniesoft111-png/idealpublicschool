25 Mar

ANNUAL ACADEMIC & SPORTS FELICITATION DAY

  • Time: 10:00 AM - 1:30 PM
  • Venue: Campus Auditorium, Ideal Public School Aranda

The Annual Felicitation Ceremony at Ideal Public School Aranda is a proud tradition celebrating our scholars, athletes, and artists who demonstrated outstanding diligence throughout the academic year. Over 150 medals, certificates of merit, and trophies were distributed to top achievers across classes Nursery to VIII.

Director Arshi Kabir and Secretary Nizamuddin Ahmad addressed the gathering, applauding the students' tenacity and commending the dedication of our teaching staff. Special recognitions were awarded for 100% attendance, Olympiad qualification, and best house leadership.

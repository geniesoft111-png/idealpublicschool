OFFICIAL NOTIFICATION: SESSION 2026-27 ADMISSIONS & RTE 25% FREE QUOTA

By Administrative Office // Date: Session 2026-27 // Category: Official Circular

Ideal Public School Aranda hereby notifies all parents, guardians, and local residents that registration for general admissions and the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act 2009 Section 12(1)(c) quota for Academic Session 2026-27 is now actively open.

RTE Quota Key Highlights:

  • Registration No.: RTE/SWN/2014-15 0079 & 21812452021924143959
  • Eligibility: Children belonging to economically weaker sections (EWS), disadvantaged groups, and families resident in the neighborhood of Aranda, Hasanpura block.
  • Reservation Quota: 25% of all entry seats in Nursery and Class I are completely free of tuition fees.
  • Required Documentation: Valid Income Certificate / BPL Card, Caste Certificate (if applicable), Proof of Residence (Aadhaar/Voter Card), and Child's Birth Certificate.

General Admission Guidelines:

General admissions are also accepted across Nursery to Class VIII on a first-come, first-served basis subject to seat availability. Parents can obtain the official prospectus and registration form from the administrative desk between 6:00 AM and 1:00 PM (Summer) or submit an inquiry through our portal.
